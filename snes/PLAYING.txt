SECRET OF GOKU - SNES {{VERSION}}

Secret of Mana. With Goku.

This is the standalone SNES cartridge port of Secret of Goku. Play with
Goku's punches, ki blasts, Kamehameha, local flight, Instant Transmission
and Super Saiyan transformation inside Secret of Mana's action RPG.

Version {{VERSION}} brings much more of the browser edition to the cartridge.
Its later story chapters, set pieces and boss battles now run natively.
Punches and kicks land with a graded hit stop, impact flashes and a
camera kick. The sounds were rebuilt around compact samples of the PC
edition's Kamehameha, Instant Transmission, charging and aura, with
crunchier hits, whooshing swings and natural variation. The original
game's weapon-charge sounds no longer play for Goku. To make room for the
samples, 17 songs use a slightly shorter echo. Ki shots and the charging
aura no longer turn into damage numbers, and flight no longer gets stuck
among flowers and trees. Crowded scenes and the largest battles can still
slow down.

APPLY THE PATCH

1. Start with an unmodified Secret of Mana (USA) ROM, original retail
   revision, with no 512-byte copier header. Keep an untouched backup.
2. Verify the base ROM against the size and hashes below. IPS itself does
   not identify a wrong base ROM, so the hash check matters.
3. Apply {{PATCH_NAME}} to that ROM with an IPS-compatible patcher such as
   Rom Patcher JS: https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/
   Do not apply this patch on top of a previous Secret of Goku build.
4. Save the result as Secret of Goku.sfc. The result must be 8,388,608 bytes
   (8 MiB), with the output SHA-256 listed below. No separate expansion or
   additional patches are needed.
5. Open the resulting .sfc in a SNES emulator that supports ExHiROM and
   32 KiB save RAM. This release was tested in Mesen 2. Physical-console
   and flash-cartridge compatibility have not been certified.

CONTROLS - ORIGINAL SNES BUTTON LABELS

  D-pad       Move
  B           Punch / talk / confirm
  Y           Ki blast
  A + D-pad   Fly fast
  X           Ring menu / back
  Hold L      Focus / build power
  L + Y       Charge Kamehameha; release to fire
  L + B       Instant Transmission
  L + A       Transform / revert
  L + R       World flight where available
  Start       Start / resume
  Select      Switch party member

In world flight, A ascends and B descends/lands. L+A and L+B keep their
Goku actions without changing altitude. Start opens the globe; R switches
to the flat map, L returns to the globe, and B closes the viewer once it
has finished loading. Native map loading takes several seconds.

Primm's Analyzer spell also shows its target's power level above it for a
few seconds. The cartridge has no scouter (the browser edition keeps one).

Goku's powers become available after his opening arrival. Use the game's
normal save points and retain the emulator's battery-save file. Browser
checkpoints and emulator save states are not interchangeable with this ROM.
Do not assume save states from an older build work after updating.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Dense scenes still have CPU slowdowns, most noticeably while charging
beams in crowded fields and in the largest battles. In the project's
gameplay benchmarks the cartridge drops no more frames than the browser
edition, and the reviewed scenarios have no observed palette mismatches,
sprite overflow or vblank overruns. The automated suite also checks for
video-memory writes outside vertical blank and other habits that real
hardware does not forgive. These measurements do not cover every map,
scripted scene or physical device.

Analyzer power readings can briefly hide while crowded scenes or effects
use the available sprite memory and frame time. Less common status and
equipment interactions still need further review. Detailed status and
test results:
https://github.com/mikolalysenko/secret-of-goku/blob/snes-v{{VERSION}}/docs/agents/snes-port/gameplay-performance.md
