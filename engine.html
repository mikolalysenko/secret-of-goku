THE SAIYAN ADVANTAGE

CONTROLS

Arrows: move / navigate menus. X: punch / hold combo / confirm. C: ki blasts.

Z + direction: fast local flight. A: ring / back / close. Up/down changes ring levels.

Shift: focus. Shift+C: charge wave. Shift+X: teleport. Hold Shift+Z: transform; tap to revert.

Shift+D: take off. D: hold to fast-forward prose and cutscenes, stopping at choices.

In world flight: Z ascends, X descends to land, Shift+C charges the wave.

SNES: B punch, Y ki, A fast flight, X ring. L focuses; L+B teleports, L+Y charges, L+A transforms, L+R takes off. R fast-forwards.

Space / Enter: Start. S: Select / switch party. Backspace: back / delete. Esc: browser pause.
