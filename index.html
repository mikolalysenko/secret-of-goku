Controls & game options

ENTER is the SNES Start button. Use E or J to confirm choices and advance dialogue. The original name entry, intro, save menus and story run normally.

R Super Saiyan · F Fly / land. In flight: arrows steer, hold J for Kamehameha, release to fire; X ascends, F descends and lands.

Tap attack repeatedly to chain punches and a finishing kick. Move while holding attack to charge and aim the wave. Hold C for a ki barrage. SHIFT + C teleports beside one enemy; attack, then press it again for the next target. Super Saiyan charges faster and fires faster. TAB switches party members; , / . are SNES L / R.

Connect a controller, then press a button.

Controller: left stick / D-pad move; south (A / cross) attacks and charges; east (B / circle) opens or closes the ring; west (X / square) fires ki; north (Y / triangle) talks and confirms. Left bumper flies fast; right bumper transforms; left trigger teleports; right trigger flies / lands. Menu starts / resumes, View switches party members, and pressing the right stick pauses.

Touch: slide across the direction pad to move diagonally. Hold the fist to charge; keep another finger on the direction pad to aim. The chevrons fly fast, rocket flies / lands, lightning transforms, streaking orb fires ki, paired arrows teleport, speech bubble confirms, and ring opens the menu. Triangle starts, two people switch party members, and bars pause.
