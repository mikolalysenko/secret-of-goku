Controls & game options

Touch: slide the D-pad to move, including diagonals. Punch also talks, confirms and lands in world flight. Hold Fly to move fast or ascend. Ki fires blasts; hold Beam to charge Kamehameha and release to fire. Hold Power to transform, or tap to revert. Menu opens the ring or goes back.

Start begins the game and accepts your name. More reveals Warp, Take off, Focus, Skip and Party; Back returns to the main controls. Each technique needs only one action button, so your other thumb can keep moving.

Keyboard & controller

Use ARROWS to move and navigate menus: left/right rotates the ring; up/down changes ring levels. A opens the ring, backs out of a submenu, or closes it. X punches, talks and confirms; mash or hold for a combo. Hold Z with an arrow to fly fast; Z alone stays still. C fires ki blasts; hold for rapid fire.

Hold SHIFT (SNES L) to focus and break out of sleep or petrification. Add C to charge Kamehameha, then release to fire; movement stays available at half speed. Add X for Instant Transmission. Hold SHIFT + Z to build the aura and transform at full power; tap the chord in Super Saiyan to return to normal. SHIFT + D takes off into world flight, punching through indoor ceilings.

In world flight, arrows steer, Z ascends and X descends to land. SHIFT + C still charges the aerial Kamehameha. SPACE starts and accepts your name; S is Select / switch party. ENTER also starts, BACKSPACE goes back / deletes a letter, and ESC pauses the browser player.

Hold D (SNES R) to fast-forward dialogue and cutscenes. Normal prose advances automatically; choices wait for your answer. Release to resume normal speed and sound.

Connect a controller, then press a button.

SNES controller layout: south B punches/confirms, west Y fires ki, east A flies fast/ascends, north X opens the ring or goes back. D-pad left/right rotates the ring; up/down changes levels. L holds focus; L+Y charges Kamehameha, L+B teleports, L+A transforms/reverts, L+R takes off. R alone fast-forwards scenes. In world flight, B descends to land. Start starts; Select switches party. No triggers or stick clicks are required.

Checkpoints are saved in this browser. The supplied ROM is never changed.
