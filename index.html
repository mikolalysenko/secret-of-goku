Controls & game options

ENTER starts and accepts your name. J punches, talks and confirms; mash or hold for a combo. E only talks/confirms. Q / X opens the ring or goes back; BACKSPACE also deletes letters. TAB switches party members.

Hold SHIFT to focus. Add C to charge Kamehameha, then release to fire; movement stays available at half speed. Add J for Instant Transmission. Hold R to build the aura and transform at full power; tap it in Super Saiyan to return to normal. C alone fires ki blasts; hold for rapid fire.

K flies fast locally. F takes off into world flight, punching through indoor ceilings. In flight, arrows steer, K ascends and J descends to land. SHIFT + C still charges the aerial Kamehameha.

Hold V to fast-forward dialogue and cutscenes. Normal prose advances automatically; choices wait for your answer. Release to resume normal speed and sound.

Connect a controller, then press a button.

SNES controller layout: south B punches/confirms, west Y fires ki, east A flies fast/ascends, north X opens the ring. L holds focus; L+Y charges Kamehameha, L+B teleports, L+A transforms/reverts, L+R takes off. R alone fast-forwards scenes. In world flight, B descends to land. Start starts; Select switches party. No triggers or stick clicks are required. Keyboard , / . also supply L / R.

Touch: fist punches, orb fires ki, chevrons fly fast, and ring opens the menu. Hold focus with orb to charge a wave or with fist to teleport. Hold lightning to transform; tap to revert. Rocket takes off, double triangles fast-forward, speech bubble confirms. In world flight, hold fist to descend and chevrons to ascend.
